# Selected wedding photos

Please place the original-resolution files in this folder using these names:

- `hero-garden.jpg` — the wide landscape garden portrait under the white arch (best hero image; both faces remain visible on desktop and mobile crops).
- `story-traditional.jpg` — the playful full-length portrait in the yellow traditional outfits (strongest image for the short story panel and adds Vietnamese character).
- `rsvp-save-the-date.jpg` — the horizontal grey-background portrait holding “Save The Date” and “For Love” signs (best natural RSVP call-to-action).
- `gallery-door.jpg` — the vertical outdoor portrait in front of the arched wooden door.
- `gallery-studio.jpg` — the close grey-background portrait where the couple are smiling and pointing.
- `gallery-traditional.jpg` — the composed traditional-outfit portrait with the lotus vase between the couple.

Avoid using the duplicate exports and the solo portraits in the first version. They are good photographs, but the invitation will feel more personal and cohesive if the first screen and key calls-to-action consistently show the couple together.
